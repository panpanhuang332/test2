文法小教室:三種名詞

可數 / 不可數 / 恆複數

種類例字動詞怎麼配
可數名詞
Countable		location(s), manufacturer(s), office(s), employee(s)Our company is small. Those two companies are bigger than ours.
不可數名詞
Uncountable		furniture, plastic, luggage, paper(紙這種材料)Plastic is easily recycled.
沒有複數形,動詞用單數!
恆複數名詞
Plural Only		headquarters, clothes, goods, congratulationsI hope that company's goods are not expensive.
  • 不可數名詞加上「量詞」就可以數了:
    Those three pieces of furniture are perfect for my bedroom.(三件家具)
    Are there a few sheets of paper in the copier?(幾張紙)
    Our four sets of luggage are too old to use.(四組行李)
  • ⚠️ 常見錯誤:a furniture ✘ → a piece of furniture ✔

聽力理解 Listening Comprehension

是非題 T / F(課本 p.58)

  • 1. Harvest Furniture Corporation currently also makes plastic. → F(以前是塑膠製造商,「現在」生產家具)
  • 2. The company was started by two people. → T(Paul 和 John Harvest 兩人創立)
  • 3. There are 2,000 employees working at the location in California. → F(加州這個據點只有 150 多人;2,000 多人是「全公司」)
